# Awareness library cover art

Unlike the capability card photography, these four covers are original artwork drawn for this
repository. They are hand-authored SVG — no stock source, no third-party licence, no attribution
owed — so the source of every asset in the repository stays traceable.

| File | Guide | Subject |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `first-hour.svg` | The first hour after you have been scammed | A dial with the first hour swept out of its edge |
| `payment-redirection.svg` | Payment redirection: a checklist for small teams | An invoice whose bank details branch to a second account |
| `phone-scams.svg` | Talking to family about phone scams | An unknown caller, and two people talking it through |
| `volunteer-organisation.svg` | Protecting a volunteer-run organisation | A committee around one shared set of controls |

Each is drawn on a 880×760 canvas with the subject centred near 42% of the height, because a card
covers the lower part of the cover with its frosted shelf and a subject centred on the canvas would
be cropped out from under the reader.

They are vector rather than raster for two reasons: each file is a few kilobytes where an equivalent
photograph is tens, and they stay sharp on the article masthead, which renders the same asset at
roughly four times the size the card does.

All four are decorative. They restate the heading in pictures and carry nothing the title and
summary do not, so every renderer sets them with `alt=""` rather than describing a drawing to a
screen reader.
